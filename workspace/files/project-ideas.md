# Project Ideas

## AI Writing Assistant
A markdown editor with built-in AI suggestions. Users can highlight text and ask for improvements, translations, or summaries.

## Knowledge Base
Organize team documents in a tree structure. Add tags, search, and link related notes together.

## Daily Standup Bot
Automated standup collection via chat. Summarizes blockers and progress for the whole team.

## Research Aggregator
Collect articles and papers on a topic. AI generates a structured summary with key findings.
